---
title: "How to grind coffee for a French press"
description: "The right grind for a French press without sludge or bitterness."
pubDate: "2026-08-20"
---
A French press needs a coarse grind, about like sea salt. Grind finer and the coffee slips through the metal filter, leaving the cup muddy and bitter.

## Ratio

Use 60 grams of coffee per liter of water.

## Time

Four minutes. Break the crust at one minute, skim the foam, and press slowly.

## Water

Just off the boil, around 200°F.
