title: "Washed vs natural coffee: what's the difference?"
description: "How processing after the harvest changes what's in your cup."
pubDate: "2026-09-10"
---
After harvest, coffee cherries are processed to get to the bean, and the process changes the flavor a lot.

## Washed

The fruit is removed and the beans are fermented and washed. The cup is clean, with bright acidity.

## Natural

The whole cherry dries in the sun for weeks. The bean takes on the fruit's sugars: sweeter, with berry notes and more body.
